## Research contribution

The paper introduced CM-PharmE as a business-architecture-informed conceptual model grounded in UFO for representing organizational, collaborative, operational, governance/regulatory, and digital-transformation concerns across pharmaceutical ecosystems.

It reported a PRISMA-guided review and thematic synthesis leading to the five-domain conceptual structure and presented the model as a basis for ontology-oriented system design, governance, collaboration, digital-health alignment, and subsequent reference-architecture work.

## Historical evaluation evidence

The paper reported qualitative evaluation across five dimensions:

- structural correctness;
- semantic accuracy;
- conceptual clarity;
- adaptability;
- pragmatic value.

Some early evaluation statements are stronger than the evidential wording used in the later journal manuscript. The repository therefore preserves these results as **historical publication evidence** and does not automatically treat them as current repository-reproduced results.

Examples include the reported `8/10` clarity result, easy customization across contexts, and participant actionability. B4 explicitly tracks these claims in [`../../evaluation/evidence/publication-evidence-matrix.csv`](../../evaluation/evidence/publication-evidence-matrix.csv).
