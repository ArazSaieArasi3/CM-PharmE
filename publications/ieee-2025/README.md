## Research Context Preserved from the Original README

The initial research framed pharmaceutical ecosystems as multi-layered interactions across organizational, regulatory, and digital domains and proposed a business-architecture-informed conceptual framework grounded in the Unified Foundational Ontology (UFO). The model was presented as extensible and intended to support ontology-based system design, strategic governance, policy development, digital-health alignment, and inter-organizational collaboration.
