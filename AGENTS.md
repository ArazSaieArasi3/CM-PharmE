# CM-PharmE collaboration and incremental release conventions

These repository conventions record Araz's explicit 2026-10-28 direction and
accepted decisions. Follow newer direct user instructions if they supersede them.

- On 2026-10-30 Araz explicitly set comprehensive **2.2** as the next target:
  preserve/integrate all valid V2 content, disposition isolated concepts/domains
  through justified semantics, reconcile model/requirement/mapping/evaluation
  gaps, and make all documentation/diagrams describe that same complete model.
  A smaller isolated capability/profile must not be reported as completion of
  this target. Retain the immutable 2.1.0 M1 release and prior acceptance history;
  execute the comprehensive work through bounded packets, without silently
  dropping domains or copying older experiments over later native decisions.

- PAGES-CUTOVER-22-01 (Araz, 2026-10-30) authorizes removing the published
  2.0/2.1 Pages model content and replacing it with accepted complete 2.2 at one
  verified cutover. This overrides older Pages path-retention rules for v2/v2-m1;
  do not remove working content before a complete validated replacement exists.
  Preserve source/release/tag/evidence history and unrelated V1. Do not ask again
  for this publication permission; verify actual full-model/candidate readiness.

- Read `v2/release/current.json` and its current approved planning record first.
  Older versioned review/CI records describe their own pinned scope; do not rewrite
  them to manufacture current acceptance.
- Work toward the smallest coherent supported release. Use MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH
  according to semantic/data contract compatibility. Research, application and
  documentation ambition does not all have to land in one ontology version.
- Prefer one S or M- packet: one decision, association, requirement, mapping,
  profile, task or small page cluster. Keep one active execution and at most three
  ready child issues. Split scope expansion before undertaking additional work.
- The assistant owns research, decision preparation, authorized implementation
  and appropriate verification. Present a concrete recommended option, advantages,
  costs and approval effect early when a genuinely new decision blocks progress.
  Do not ask again for D-01/SC-REL/D-03, which Araz explicitly accepted.
- RelComp RC-A01/RC-A02 acceptance covers only the recorded named bounded profile.
  It does not accept the other three source policies, all ORSD rows, full native
  validation, external experts or GA. Preserve all evidence/engine/oracle history.
- Keep core identity/meaning reusable globally. Local country/source/application
  completeness and SQL/SHACL admission requirements belong in explicit profiles
  unless a universal semantic restriction is justified. Do not guess unknowns.
- Apply the current precise license scope to project-owned assets; retain external
  data/software notices and keep already published payloads unchanged.
- After 2–4 accepted packets, inspect the smallest eligible release. Publish only
  a meaningful change with its applicable checks and exact immutable receipt.
- Keep user-facing Persian checkpoint reports short: current version/objective,
  completed issue/result, and a compact next-work/release-point table. Repository
  content is English. Broader detail belongs in the committed evidence record.
- Do not contact experts/other people or invent their responses/consent/approval.
  Preserve source-qualified, synthetic and unknown evidence distinctions.
