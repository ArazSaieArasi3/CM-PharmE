## Why this distinction matters

A credible V2 contribution must distinguish what is inherited from V1 from what is refined and what is genuinely new. Repackaging prior work as novelty would weaken the research argument.

## Continuity — foundations retained

The following are important continuities and are not V2 novelty by themselves:
- pharmaceutical ecosystem as the research scope;
- ecosystem actors/relationships as a central concern;
- governance/regulatory concern;
- supply/demand and process/activity concern;
- UFO/OntoUML methodological lineage;
- conceptual modeling and relation semantics;
- competency-question practice;
- repository/traceability/reproducibility experience;
- selected risk, digital, clinical and business-architecture concerns.

## Refinement / restructuring

V2 materially refines prior concerns by:
- replacing enterprise-centric identity with Organization plus contextual roles;
- separating Organization and physical Facility;
- replacing generic ecosystem relationships with typed relators/relations;
- splitting generic business process into evidence-backed activity types;
- separating capacity from observations about capacity;
- moving technology-specific constructs away from Core;
- isolating Business Architecture as an Extension/view;
- reorganizing five broad domains into Core/X-INFRA/Extensions.

These are changes in conceptual architecture, not simply additional labels.

## New V2 semantic contributions

Current evidence supports novelty claims around the introduction or first-class treatment of:
- Medicinal Product–Substance–Presentation–Dosage Form–Strength–Package structure;
- explicit classification Scheme–Entry–Assignment;
- Regulatory Jurisdiction versus geography;
- shortage situation versus source/evidence record;
- Observation Activity versus persistent Observation Result;
- first-class Dataset–Release–Record–Assertion–Evidence–Provenance semantics;
- Identifier Scheme/Value/Assignment and auditable entity-match semantics;
- modular resilience/market-access/risk extensions;
- ontology-aligned PostgreSQL/PostGIS and deterministic RDF/KG representation;
- prospective held-out and cross-representation evaluation.

## Methodological/engineering novelty versus domain-semantic novelty

Not every V2 contribution is a new pharmaceutical-domain concept.

Separate:
- **domain-semantic novelty** — new/refined distinctions about pharmaceutical entities/relations;
- **methodological novelty** — evidence admission, held-out design, human review;
- **representational/engineering contribution** — OWL/SHACL, RDB/KG mapping, reproducible pipelines;
- **application evidence** — bounded demonstrator tasks.

This separation prevents engineering scale from being misrepresented as ontological novelty.

## Claim boundaries

Do not claim:
- a percentage improvement in ecosystem coverage over V1;
- universal domain completeness;
- stronger expert validation before E9 collection;
- application effectiveness before Gate G evidence;
- real-world entity-resolution performance without a labeled benchmark.
