## What is CM-PharmE?

CM-PharmE is a versioned, ontology-grounded research program for representing and analyzing the pharmaceutical ecosystem through explicit conceptual structures, formal semantics, evidence traceability and progressively richer data/application realizations.

## Versioned research program

### CM-PharmE 1.x

The V1 line established the stable published conceptual foundation and its repository-supported research, formalization, evaluation and reproducibility package. Its selected documentation authority is `main`.

### CM-PharmE 2.0

V2 is an active research evolution rather than a replacement narrative that erases V1. It expands the evidence base, conceptual architecture, formal ontology engineering, provenance-aware data realization, multi-family evaluation, human ontology review and application/demonstrator layers. Its selected authority is `v2/research-program`.

V2 is **not final** at this documentation point.

## Documentation principle

The Wiki presents a coherent research story while preserving:
- version boundaries;
- evidence boundaries;
- continuity versus novelty;
- limitations and negative/partial findings;
- links back to authoritative repository artifacts.
