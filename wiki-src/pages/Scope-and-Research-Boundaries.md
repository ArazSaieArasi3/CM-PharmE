## Overall scope

CM-PharmE investigates the pharmaceutical ecosystem at conceptual, ontological, evidence, data-representation and selected application-demonstration levels.

It is not a claim that one model replaces every regulatory, clinical, product, supply, finance or implementation standard.

## V1 scope

V1 focuses on an integrated ecosystem conceptual model informed by business-architecture concerns and grounded through UFO/OntoUML modeling commitments, then supported by formalization, evaluation and reproducibility artifacts.

## V2 scope

V2 develops a data-grounded UFO/OntoUML ontology and knowledge infrastructure with:
- heterogeneous evidence/data grounding;
- explicit provenance, geography, time and jurisdiction;
- modular Core/X-INFRA/Extension organization;
- OWL/SHACL formalization;
- ontology-aligned relational/KG realization;
- prospective multi-family evaluation;
- human review;
- bounded demonstrators.

V2 remains evolving.

## Persistent non-claims

Unless dedicated evidence is available, the project does not claim:
- universal ecosystem completeness;
- complete global product→supplier→buyer→shipment reconstruction;
- production-scale deployment effectiveness;
- regulatory/legal compliance;
- predictive or causal resilience performance;
- general AI novelty/performance;
- real-world entity-resolution accuracy;
- quantitative V2 superiority over V1 without comparable denominators.

## Reading rule

Every claim should be interpreted at the scope of its own version, evidence family, dataset/fixture and evaluation protocol.
