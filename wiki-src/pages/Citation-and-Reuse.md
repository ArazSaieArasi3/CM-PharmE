## Citation principle

When using CM-PharmE in research:
1. cite the relevant scholarly publication where applicable;
2. identify the CM-PharmE model/semantic version used;
3. record the repository ref or archived research package when reproducibility matters.

The repository's machine-readable citation record is `CITATION.cff`.

## Current V1 citation metadata

The current `CITATION.cff` identifies:
- title: **CM-PharmE: An Evolving Ontology-Grounded Conceptual Model for the Pharmaceutical Ecosystem**;
- version: **1.0.0**;
- repository code location;
- authors and ORCIDs as recorded in the file.

Use the exact machine-readable record rather than reproducing bibliographic fields from memory.

## Publication citation versus artifact citation

A publication citation answers “which study?”  
A model/repository citation answers “which exact artifact/version?”

For reproducible research, both may be necessary.

## V2 reuse

V2 is currently evolving and does not yet have a final research-release/DOI citation package.

Until Gate H:
- identify `v2/research-program` and an exact commit/ref;
- do not cite a nonexistent final V2 release;
- do not label `2.0.0-alpha.1` as the final CM-PharmE 2.0 research release.

## Wiki citation

Wiki baseline identifiers such as `WB-2026.09.1` identify documentation snapshots only. They are not semantic model versions.

A citable/frozen Wiki snapshot will be governed through #217 and linked to the final research package where appropriate.

## Reuse boundaries

Reuse should preserve:
- version scope;
- provenance;
- limitations/non-claims;
- distinction between ontology semantics and implementation-specific structures.
