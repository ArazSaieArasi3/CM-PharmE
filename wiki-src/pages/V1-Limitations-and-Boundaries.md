# V1 Limitations and Boundaries

> **Version scope:** V1  
> **Status:** Stable  
> **Updated:** 2026-09-23

## Why limitations are first-class documentation

CM-PharmE V1 is a research conceptual model and formal/repository artifact. Evaluation supports specific claims under specific procedures; it does not convert the model into a universally complete or production-proven ontology.

## Core evidence boundaries

V1 does not by itself establish:
- universal pharmaceutical-ecosystem completeness;
- legal or regulatory compliance;
- exact FHIR, IDMP, BACM or other external-standard conformance;
- production deployment feasibility;
- migration cost or organizational adoption;
- clinical effectiveness;
- universal correctness of every ontological commitment;
- empirical effectiveness of every proposed application.

## Formal-ontology boundary

Logical reasoning success demonstrates processability/consistency for the evaluated axiom set. It does not prove conceptual correctness or domain completeness.

The repository also distinguishes OWL-profile hygiene findings from logical inconsistency.

## Semantic-review boundary

V1 preserves known review flags, including stereotype/mediation ambiguity and historical conversion artifacts, rather than silently rewriting the frozen baseline.

## Evaluation boundary

Some evaluation layers are PARTIAL or CONDITIONAL. Illustrative scenario evidence and repository reproducibility must not be generalized to real-world operational performance.

## Business-architecture boundary

Business architecture is an analytical lens for concern identification. V1 does not claim exhaustive BACM conformance.

## Domain-architecture boundary

The five domains are modeling domains, not inherently DDD bounded contexts, microservices, enterprise departments or universal pharmaceutical taxonomy partitions.

## Evolution boundary

V2 may address evidence gaps or introduce new architecture, but V2 evidence must not be retroactively used to make V1 appear stronger or weaker than its own evidence horizon.

See [[V1 to V2 Research Evolution]] and [[Cross-Version Comparison Limits]].
