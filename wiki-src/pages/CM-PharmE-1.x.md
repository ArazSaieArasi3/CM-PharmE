## Purpose

CM-PharmE 1.x is the stable conceptual and repository-supported baseline from which the later V2 research program evolves. This page is the entry point to V1 as a self-contained research artifact; V2 is not required to understand V1 semantics.

## Research identity

V1 addresses ecosystem-level semantic fragmentation across pharmaceutical enterprises, regulators, healthcare actors, collaborative arrangements, operational processes, governance constraints and digital enablers.

Its modeling logic combines:
1. PRISMA-guided evidence review and thematic synthesis;
2. business-architecture-informed concern identification;
3. UFO/OntoUML classification and relation design;
4. formal ontology engineering and executable repository validation;
5. versioned evidence and reproducibility controls.

## Stable semantic baseline

The stable semantic release is `v1.0.0`.

Repository statistics for that baseline:
- 39 canonical concepts;
- 40 canonical semantic relations;
- 5 architectural domains;
- concept stereotypes: 13 kind, 8 mode, 7 role, 6 relator, 5 perdurant.

See [[V1 Conceptual Model]], [[V1 Concepts]], [[V1 Relations]] and [[V1 Domain Architecture]].

## Research and evaluation story

V1 should be read as a sequence:

**Problem framing → evidence review → thematic synthesis → five-domain decomposition → business-architecture concern identification → UFO/OntoUML conceptualization → relation/cardinality design → formalization → demonstration → layered evaluation → reproducibility**

See [[V1 Research Design and Evidence Review]] and [[V1 Evaluation and Evidence]].

## Formal and engineering layer

The historical OWL export is preserved for provenance, while the repository also maintains a cleaned canonical formal source and reproducible engineering pipeline.

## Publication lineage

## Boundaries
