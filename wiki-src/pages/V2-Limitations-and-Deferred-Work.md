# V2 Limitations and Deferred Work

> **Version scope:** V2  
> **Status:** Stable-to-date / Evolving  
> **Updated:** 2026-09-23

## Persistent limitations

The current V2 evidence does not support:
1. complete global product→supplier→buyer→shipment reconstruction;
2. full external dataset ingestion or global data completeness;
3. production-scale API/application deployment effectiveness;
4. universal cross-jurisdiction generalizability;
5. AI novelty/performance, GraphRAG utility or autonomous semantic correctness;
6. predictive shortage/risk/resilience claims;
7. causal intervention-effectiveness claims;
8. real-world entity-resolution precision/recall/F1;
9. official OntoUML-tool certification;
10. exact conformance to external standards or a separate risk ontology without dedicated alignment evaluation;
11. quantitative V2 superiority over V1 without comparable denominators;
12. expert-validation results before actual E9 collection.

## E9

The expert protocol is ready, but empirical human evidence is absent. This is a real limitation, not a documentation gap that can be filled by inference.

## Held-out/generalizability boundary

E8 provides useful bounded evidence across selected held-out source families but cannot support global-domain completeness.

## Formalization boundary

Selected extension semantics still carry warnings/gaps, particularly around some mediation, Mode bearer, recovery and risk-treatment semantics.

## W8 boundary

Successful rendering or deterministic task execution is not equivalent to demonstrated usability/effectiveness.

Representative-task evaluation #170 remains open and Gate G is not frozen.

## Human-review boundary

#159/#173 may lead to semantic refinements after explicit author disposition. Until then, findings/proposals remain review evidence rather than canonical ontology changes.

## Deferred work

Deferred/future work includes:
- real E9 expert collection/analysis;
- completion of W8 representative tasks and Gate G;
- final concept/relation evidence-review cycle;
- final manuscript traceability audit;
- reproducible research/DOI release;
- target-journal freeze;
- Gate H final V2 freeze.
