# Applications and Boundaries

> **Version scope:** Cross-version  
> **Status:** Stable-to-date / Evolving  
> **Updated:** 2026-09-23

## Application role in CM-PharmE

Applications are used to expose and test model/ontology/data capabilities. They do not automatically become evidence that the ontology is complete, commercially useful, clinically effective or production ready.

## V1 application pathways

V1 identifies possible uses including:
- ecosystem/governance reasoning;
- requirements engineering;
- interoperability-oriented analysis;
- reference-architecture interpretation;
- semantic mapping;
- knowledge-graph development;
- dataset mapping;
- future software/data implementations.

These are pathways, not deployed-effectiveness claims.

## V2 demonstrator strategy

V2 distinguishes:
1. Research Demonstrators — evaluated in the research program.
2. Application Candidates — may be prototyped.
3. Future Research/Product Opportunities — outside principal article scope.

Current W8 surfaces include actor/facility mapping, entity/relationship browsing, KG exploration, governed analytics, resilience scenarios and provenance-aware semantic search.

## Evidence boundary

A demonstrator may support claims about:
- representability;
- deterministic task correctness;
- provenance visibility;
- bounded query/analytics behavior.

It does not by itself support:
- user effectiveness/usability;
- production reliability;
- predictive validity;
- global data completeness;
- real-world entity-resolution performance;
- AI novelty/performance.
