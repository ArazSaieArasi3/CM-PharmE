## Interpretation boundary
These results support reproducibility, selected structural integrity, OWL 2 DL profile compliance and bounded logical consistency of the current axiom set. They do not establish domain completeness, empirical effectiveness, data quality, mapping accuracy, cross-jurisdiction generalizability or application performance; those require W6/W7 evidence.
