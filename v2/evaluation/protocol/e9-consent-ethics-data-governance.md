# W7-E9 Consent, Ethics and Data-Governance Boundary

## Status
This document defines the planned minimal-risk expert-review handling for CM-PharmE 2.0. It does **not** assert ethics approval, waiver or exemption. Any applicable university/institutional requirement must be checked before recruitment and recorded if applicable.

## Consent text to provide before participation
Participants are informed that:
- participation is voluntary;
- the purpose is academic evaluation of a pharmaceutical ecosystem ontology;
- they may stop before submitting their response without penalty;
- only professional-profile information needed to establish eligibility is requested;
- published/repository results will not identify them by name unless they separately and explicitly consent to attribution;
- anonymized quotations may be used only when they cannot reasonably identify the participant;
- responses will be analyzed in aggregate and by broad expertise stratum when feasible.

Consent must be explicitly recorded before substantive responses are accepted.

## Minimum participant metadata
Collect only:
- anonymous participant ID;
- expertise stratum(s);
- broad role category (academic / industry / regulator / healthcare / other);
- years of relevant experience band (`3-5`, `6-10`, `11-20`, `>20`);
- highest relevant qualification category, if volunteered/required for eligibility;
- country/region at broad level only if needed for jurisdiction-diversity reporting;
- conflict-of-interest declaration;
- consent status/date.

Do not collect personal identifiers in the analysis dataset unless operationally necessary for recruitment administration.

## Separation of identifiers
If contact details are needed for recruitment, keep them outside the analysis dataset. The repository should contain only templates and anonymized/aggregated expert evidence; raw identifiable contact data must not be committed to the public repository.

## Participant IDs
Use stable pseudonymous IDs such as `EX-DOM-01`, `EX-ONT-01` or `EX-BOTH-01`. The mapping from IDs to real identities, if it exists, stays outside the public repository and is access-restricted.

## Withdrawal
Before anonymized aggregation/publication, a participant may request removal of their response if operationally feasible under the applicable consent process. The exact cut-off used for the study must be communicated during recruitment.

## Quotation rule
Do not publish a verbatim comment that contains identifying information. Paraphrase or redact when necessary. Any attributed quotation requires separate explicit permission.

## Conflict of interest
Participants disclose whether they have a direct authorship, supervisory, commercial or other relationship that may materially affect independence. Direct CM-PharmE 2.0 authors are not counted as independent experts.

## Repository boundary
Public repository may contain:
- frozen protocol/instrument;
- empty response/register templates;
- anonymized item-level responses if ethically and institutionally appropriate;
- aggregated statistics;
- anonymized finding codes;
- deviation and adjudication logs.

Public repository must not contain:
- private email addresses/phone numbers;
- signed consent forms with personal identifiers;
- identity keys linking participant IDs to names;
- sensitive personal information.

## Manuscript boundary
Until real responses are collected, the manuscript can describe only the planned/frozen prospective expert-evaluation method. It must not report expert results, sample characteristics or conclusions that have not actually been observed.
