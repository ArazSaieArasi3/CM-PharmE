C-07, C-10, C-11, C-12, C-14, C-15, C-16, C-17, C-19, C-20, C-21, C-22, C-25, C-27 and C-30 are approved exactly within the safe boundaries already recorded in the authoritative claim-evidence register.

### Defer performance/effectiveness claims
- **C-08:** geospatial demonstrator representability/provenance/query mechanics may be described, but demonstrator usability/effectiveness remains deferred to W8.
- **C-32:** cross-source entity-match representation/audit mechanics may be described, but real-world entity-resolution precision/recall remains deferred until separately evaluated.

## E9 expert-evidence boundary
E9 remains open and operationally launch-ready, but there are still zero real eligible expert responses and zero expert-result claims. Therefore:
- no expert-validation result enters current Results wording;
- no synthetic, inferred or reconstructed response is admissible;
- E9-dependent claims remain deferred until real data are collected/analyzed under the frozen protocol;
- later insertion of E9 evidence is an additive evaluation-family update and does not rewrite the already frozen computational evidence.

## Progression decision
**Gate F approves progression to the manuscript-facing W8/application and Paper Track workstreams under these claim boundaries.**

This approval does not close E9/#98, does not authorize unsupported application/AI performance claims, does not merge the W7 PR by itself, and does not authorize final journal submission/release. Subsequent work must preserve the claim-evidence contract and keep W8 application evaluation analytically separate from W7 ontology-evaluation evidence.

## Non-claims
