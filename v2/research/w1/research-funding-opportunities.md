## Partnership archetypes to seek later
1. **Pharmaceutical/MAH partner** — domain requirements, shortage/supply expertise, non-sensitive validation.
2. **Regulatory/public-health partner** — critical medicine, regulatory data and preparedness use cases.
3. **Academic pharmacy/pharmacoepidemiology partner** — data interpretation and domain validation.
4. **Ontology/conceptual-modeling partner** — UFO/OntoUML expert review.
5. **Data/KG/AI partner** — entity resolution, KG analytics, GraphRAG/ML evaluation.
6. **Health-economics/market-access partner** — if reimbursement/access is promoted.
7. **Supply-chain/resilience partner** — if resilience becomes a second substantial research stream.

## Exploitation / translation routes
- open research ontology and reproducible benchmark package;
- global ecosystem observatory demonstrator;
- semantic API/data service;
- regulatory-data quality/compliance tooling;
- supply/resilience intelligence demonstrator;
- future consortium proposal or funded pilot;
- separate analytics/AI follow-on papers.

## Governance cautions
- Record current calls with date and status; do not treat them as durable facts.
- Verify organization/country eligibility and consortium conditions before proposal work.
- Keep commercial/funding interest separate from scientific evidence.
- Do not place proprietary or access-restricted source data in the public repository without explicit permission.
- Prefer opportunities that naturally fit the ontology/data program rather than reshaping the research solely to match a call.

## W1 recommendation
The strongest near-term external positioning is **research infrastructure + pharmaceutical resilience + regulatory/data interoperability**, with AI as a supporting capability. Funding should be monitored continuously, but the V2 research program should proceed independently of whether a current call is pursued.
