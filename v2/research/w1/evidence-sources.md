# W1 Evidence Sources — Needs, Uses & Opportunities

Status: curated application/methodological evidence for W1. These sources support need/use-case discovery only; they are **not** dataset-admission decisions. Dataset admission is deferred to W2.

| ID | Source | Type | W1 relevance |
|---|---|---|---|
| W1-S01 | U.S. FDA — Drug Shortages | Official regulatory source | Documents shortage causes including manufacturing/quality problems, delays, raw-material/component problems, demand increases and discontinuations. Supports shortage/resilience problem framing. |
| W1-S02 | U.S. FDA — Electronic Drug Registration and Listing System / Drug Establishment Current Registration Site | Official regulatory source | Establishment registration and drug-listing infrastructure; supports organization/site identity and facility mapping needs. |
| W1-S03 | European Medicines Agency — European Shortages Monitoring Platform (ESMP) | Official regulatory platform | Supports supply, demand, availability, shortage reporting, crisis/preparedness monitoring, MAH/NCA interoperability and machine-to-machine exchange. |
| W1-S04 | European Medicines Agency — Union List of Critical Medicines and supply-chain vulnerability methodology | Official regulatory source | Supports critical-medicine prioritization, vulnerability assessment, alternative-supply reasoning and crisis/resilience use cases. |
| W1-S05 | European Medicines Agency — EudraGMDP | Official regulatory database | Public manufacturing/import, GMP/GDP, wholesale-distribution, active-substance manufacturer/distributor and site/location evidence. Supports actor/facility geospatial intelligence. |
| W1-S06 | ClinicalTrials.gov — Data/API, Study Data Structure and Search Areas | Official research registry/API | Provides sponsor, collaborator, facility, city/state/ZIP/country and trial-intervention structures. Supports sponsor/site/product network and cross-jurisdiction use cases. |
| W1-S07 | World Health Organization — Essential Medicines and Access Indicators | Official international source | Supports availability/affordability/access as measurable pharmaceutical-ecosystem concerns. |
| W1-S08 | European Medicines Agency — EudraVigilance access and system overview | Official pharmacovigilance source | Supports safety-signal, adverse-event, product normalization, stakeholder-access and provenance-aware pharmacovigilance use cases. |
| W1-S09 | Chen et al., 2026, *Knowledge graph-enhanced deep learning for pharmaceutical demand forecasting*, Scientific Reports, DOI: 10.1038/s41598-026-35113-4 | Peer-reviewed primary research | Demonstrates a concrete KG+AI demand-forecasting application and motivates future analytical/AI opportunities. |
| W1-S10 | 2026 South Korea drug-shortage prediction study, PMID 41585895 | Peer-reviewed primary research | Demonstrates predictive modeling of shortage duration and causal categories using regulatory-reported shortage cases. |
| W1-S11 | *A patient safety knowledge graph supporting vaccine product development* | Peer-reviewed primary research | Demonstrates public-data ETL, semantic harmonization, KG/API/web access and large-scale pharmacovigilance analysis. |
| W1-S12 | *Adverse Drug Event Prediction Using Noisy Literature-Derived Knowledge Graphs* | Peer-reviewed primary research | Demonstrates KG representation learning for pharmacovigilance prediction and the importance of uncertainty/noise modeling. |
| W1-S13 | Common Ontology of Value and Risk (COVER) / Reference Ontology for Security Engineering (ROSE), UFO-grounded risk literature | Peer-reviewed methodological evidence | Supports modular reuse/alignment of generic risk, vulnerability, event, value, consequence and treatment semantics rather than duplicating them in the pharmaceutical Core. |
| W1-S14 | IHI Call 13 (HORIZON-JU-IHI-2026-13-two-stage) | Current EU research/innovation opportunity | Includes a healthcare innovation network topic and an AI foundation toxicology topic. Useful as a partnership/funding landscape item; not ontology-scope evidence. |
| W1-S15 | DIGITAL-2026-AI-DATA-10-COMPLIANCE | Current EU deployment/funding opportunity | Supports data-driven digital compliance solutions; potentially relevant to semantic compliance tooling and regulatory-data applications. |
| W1-S16 | EU4Health/HaDEA — Long-term API stockpiling and rapid production of finished dose forms | Current EU preparedness procurement | Directly signals policy/operational demand for pharmaceutical resilience, stockpiling, rapid production and crisis preparedness. |
| W1-S17 | Horizon Europe Cluster 1 Health Work Programme 2026–2027 | EU research programme | Relevant to regulatory science, health data/digital tools and collaborative health research; opportunity fit must be assessed per call. |
| W1-S18 | HERA — Funding and opportunities | EU preparedness/response programme | Supports medical-countermeasure development, manufacturing, procurement, distribution and data-driven preparedness opportunity discovery. |

## Evidence boundary
W1 sources establish that the identified needs and application families are plausible and relevant. They do not establish that CM-PharmE 2.0 already satisfies those needs, nor do they admit any specific dataset into the V2 empirical design. Those claims are deferred to W2–W7.
