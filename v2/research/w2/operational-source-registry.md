# W2 Authoritative Operational-Source Registry

## Purpose
Record high-authority pharmaceutical and reference sources that may not have dataset DOIs but are important for current domain semantics, identity, implementation or held-out evaluation.

| ID | Source | Authority / access | Key structures relevant to V2 | W2 role | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| O01 | FDA Drug Establishments Current Registration Site (DECRS) | FDA; public search + annual-registration ZIP download | Drug establishment, registration status, organization/site details for facilities manufacturing/preparing/compounding/processing drugs distributed in/imported to the U.S. | **Primary operational discovery/implementation** for actor/facility identity and geospatial mapping | Does not contain WDD/3PL establishments; registration is not equivalent to product approval/compliance. |
| O02 | FDA Wholesale Drug Distributor / Third-Party Logistics Provider reporting database | FDA; search + downloadable database; business-day updates | Facility, wholesale distributor/3PL role, state license/reporting information | **Primary operational discovery** for distributor/logistics roles and sites | A reported facility may have multiple licenses; reporting is not FDA approval or proof of compliance. |
| O03 | openFDA NDC | FDA/openFDA; API + dataset download; daily updates | NDC product/package identifiers, brand/generic, active ingredients, dosage form/route, application and harmonized identifiers | **Primary product/identity enrichment** | NDC listing is labeler-submitted and does not itself mean FDA verification/approval. |
| O04 | FDA/openFDA Structured Product Labeling | FDA; HL7 SPL submissions, openFDA/original downloads | Product and facility information, labeling, identifiers, author/submitting firm | **Primary schema/identity source**; candidate product↔facility parsing | Submission data need careful interpretation; labels are not a complete supply-network representation. |
| O05 | openFDA Drug Shortages | FDA/openFDA; API/download | Shortage records and harmonized product identifiers | **Reserved held-out U.S. shortage source** | Keep out of W3 shortage concept admission if it will be used for independent cross-jurisdiction evaluation. |
| O06 | EMA Union List of Critical Medicines | EMA/HMA/EC; official XLSX, current V2.1 revision 1 | Medicine/substance groups, criticality status; methodology considers disease seriousness and alternatives | **Primary criticality discovery/reference** | Criticality is EU-contextual and must not be generalized to universal criticality without context. |
| O07 | EMA Medicine Supply Shortages JSON | EMA public website-data JSON | medicine, shortage status, INN/common name, therapeutic area, forms, strengths, alternatives, shortage start/resolution and publication/update dates | **Primary EU shortage discovery/implementation** | Represents EMA website shortage scope, not all national shortage events. |
| O08 | EMA EudraGMDP | EMA public Union database | Manufacturing/import authorisations, GMP/GDP, wholesale-distribution authorisations, active-substance manufacturer/importer/distributor registration, organization/location master data | **High-value actor/site/role semantic source** | Public bulk/API ingestion was not verified in W2; automated ingestion remains conditional on a compliant access method. |
| O09 | EMA European Shortages Monitoring Platform (ESMP) reporting model | EMA operational platform/guidance/templates | Supply, demand, availability, shortage reporting, MAH/NCA roles and machine-to-machine exchange | **Schema/methodological evidence** for shortage observations and reporting roles | Not treated as a public bulk empirical dataset. |
| O10 | WHO Model List / Electronic Essential Medicines List | WHO; public authoritative medicines resource | Essential medicine, indication, formulation, classification/alternatives depending release/interface | **Primary essentiality/product semantic source** | Exact machine-readable export/reuse terms must be checked before redistribution; essentiality is context-sensitive. |
| O11 | WHO National Essential Medicines Lists repository | WHO-hosted country resources | National lists across many jurisdictions | **Candidate held-out cross-jurisdiction source** | Per-country file format, recency and reuse rights vary; select only after per-list audit. |
| O12 | ClinicalTrials.gov API/download | U.S. NLM/NIH; public API/download | NCT study, sponsor, collaborator, intervention, facility, city/state/postcode/country, study status/time | **Reserved held-out global actor/site/location/trial source** | Do not use schema to admit Core concepts if claiming it later as held-out generalizability evidence. |
| O13 | AACT | CTTI; PostgreSQL dump + flat tables; 2026-08-10 snapshot | 51-table relational rendering of ClinicalTrials.gov including sponsors, facilities, interventions, countries and study relations | **Held-out relational benchmark / later RDB comparison** | Derived from ClinicalTrials.gov; not independent from O12. Use O12/O13 as one held-out source family. |
| O14 | openFDA FAERS | FDA/openFDA; API/download | Adverse-event reports and harmonized drug fields | Optional safety extension/held-out | Spontaneous reports have reporting bias and cannot establish causal relationships. |
| O15 | ChEMBL 37 | EMBL-EBI; bulk/API; CC BY-SA 3.0 | Molecules/compounds, bioactivities, targets, identifiers | **Secondary substance/identifier enrichment** | Biomedical/drug-discovery database, not evidence of ecosystem supply/manufacturing relations. |
| G01 | GeoNames | Global gazetteer; daily dumps; CC BY 4.0 | Geoname ID, names, coordinates, country/admin codes, feature codes, alternate names | **Geospatial normalization/enrichment** | Reference geography only; must not be used as evidence for pharmaceutical-domain concept admission. |

## Reserved held-out design
Two source families are intentionally protected from W3 Core concept discovery:
1. **ClinicalTrials.gov/AACT** — external/global test of organization, facility, role, location, intervention and relational-schema generalizability.
2. **openFDA Drug Shortages** — U.S. held-out test against EU shortage/criticality semantics discovered from EMA sources.

This reservation is a methodological control: feasibility fields may be documented in W2, but the held-out schemas must not be used to justify admission of Core classes/relations that will later be counted as externally covered.
