## Completion contract

Freeze 16 explicit legacy witnesses: four-cell rigid and Role matrices, all four
reordered rigid states, optional-end and unlimited controls, and a Kind control.
Independently inspect the official parser's structural fields, readOnly values and
resolved end IDs. Replay six historical fixtures without changing bytes/counts;
retain absent-attribute/default distinctions. Refuse 12 malformed/out-of-scope
legacy inputs. Exercise seven native admission cases, including both known values,
null/missing/malformed values and refusal of the unchanged full candidate.

Native admission wraps the historical structural adapter with an exact Boolean
type guard. Passing that guard does not make its nature/metadata/full-model fidelity
sufficient. Preserve the native source's uncertainty and hash, and count inventories
as inventories rather than RelRig findings. This completes the endpoint-review slice
when independently hosted evidence passes, not final scientific adjudication.
