# Domains

CM-PharmE domains organize concepts and relationships into coherent areas of concern while preserving cross-domain interactions. Each domain will have its own stable identifier, definition, scope, concept membership, core relations, incoming/outgoing cross-domain relations, ontology notes, mappings, datasets, publications, and version history.

The v1.0.0 historical domain visualization is preserved at [`releases/v1.0.0/model/Domains-of-CM-PharmE-1.0.png`](../../releases/v1.0.0/model/Domains-of-CM-PharmE-1.0.png).
