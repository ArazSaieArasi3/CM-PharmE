# CM-PharmE Domains

CM-PharmE v1.0.0 is organized into five architectural domains. Domain membership is modeled as traceability metadata: a concept has one primary domain and may have additional cross-domain memberships.

| ID | Domain | Primary concepts | All mapped concepts |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| `CMPE-D0001` | [Organizational / Structural](organizational-structural.md) | 8 | 11 |
| `CMPE-D0002` | [Ecosystem / Collaborative](ecosystem-collaborative.md) | 9 | 13 |
| `CMPE-D0003` | [Operational / Process](operational-process.md) | 8 | 19 |
| `CMPE-D0004` | [Governance / Regulatory](governance-regulatory.md) | 7 | 13 |
| `CMPE-D0005` | [Digital Transformation](digital-transformation.md) | 7 | 7 |

The counts distinguish **primary assignment** from **cross-domain coverage**; concepts may intentionally participate in more than one domain. The historical domain image remains preserved at [`releases/v1.0.0/model/Domains-of-CM-PharmE-1.0.png`](../../releases/v1.0.0/model/Domains-of-CM-PharmE-1.0.png).
