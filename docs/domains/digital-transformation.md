# Digital Transformation

- **Stable ID:** `CMPE-D0005`
- **Status:** Active
- **Introduced in:** `v1.0.0`
- **Aliases:** Digital Transformation and Information Technology

## Scope
Digital platforms and information technologies that enable data exchange, clinical support, traceability, interoperability, and digitally mediated coordination.

## Primary concepts
`CMPE-C0029`, `CMPE-C0030`, `CMPE-C0031`, `CMPE-C0033`, `CMPE-C0034`, `CMPE-C0035`, `CMPE-C0038`.

## Cross-domain interaction
Digital constructs support operational workflows and provide information to governance, risk and pharmacovigilance activities. They are modeled as embedded ecosystem enablers rather than an isolated technology layer.
