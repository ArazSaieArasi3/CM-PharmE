# Methodology

CM-PharmE separates reusable research/modeling methods from version-specific results. The methodology pages explain how evidence is translated into modeling commitments and how those commitments are evaluated without turning the repository into a duplicate manuscript.

## Available methods

- [Research and Model Development Method](research-and-model-development.md) — problem framing, PRISMA-guided evidence review, thematic synthesis, evidence-to-domain decomposition, business-architecture concern identification, UFO/OntoUML classification, relation selection, cardinality assignment, and versioned traceability.
- [Evaluation Method](evaluation-method.md) — publication-derived quality criteria translated into repository evaluation layers E1–E9, with explicit evidence-strength boundaries.

## Method/result separation

Reusable methods are documented here. Version- or batch-specific evidence and results are maintained under [`../evaluations/`](../evaluations/) and [`../../evaluation/`](../../evaluation/).

The associated publications remain the scholarly source for the complete research narrative; these repository pages retain only the method needed to understand, reproduce, inspect, and extend CM-PharmE.
